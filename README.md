# Mobigate Group Management Portal (MGM-Portal)

Mobigate is a comprehensive Group Management and Personnel Management Portal designed to centralize the administration, monitoring, communication, and financial records of personnel across an organization. The platform enables the company to manage employee, contractor, partner, and director information in one secure digital environment while giving each person access to their personal records, communication tools, and related services.

## Project Overview

The MGM-Portal is built to support the full lifecycle of personnel management within the organization. It provides a structured system for onboarding, account approval, personnel administration, payroll-related records, insurance documentation, and digital communication between staff members.

The platform is intended to serve as a central HR and operations hub for the company, ensuring proper record keeping, accountability, transparency, and efficient communication across departments and teams.

## Core Business Scope

### 1. Personnel Administration & Management

The portal maintains a comprehensive personnel database that captures all relevant identity, employment, and operational information for each person associated with the organization.

#### Personnel Database Structure
The system stores and manages details such as:
- Full names
- Bio-data and personal information
- Office or position
- Date of employment or appointment
- Department or unit
- Remuneration and compensation package
- Job roles and specifications
- Job schedules
- Rules of engagement
- Vacation schedules
- Overtime schedules
- Supporting documentation
- Queries and reports
- Loans and IOU records
- Suspensions
- Penalties
- Clock-ins and clock-outs
- Transfers
- Promotions
- Welfare records
- Contributions
- Stakeholdings
- Recognitions and awards
- Appraisals
- Outstanding feats
- Recommendations
- Teams
- Accountability and asset custody records

This makes the portal a complete record repository for personnel administration and organizational workforce management.

#### Financial Records
The portal also stores and tracks financial details pertaining to the personnel, including:
- Salary payments
- Overtime payments
- Loans taken
- Contributions
- Other employee-related financial records and activities

#### Insurance Records
The platform supports management of insurance documentation and records, including:
- Life insurance
- Comprehensive insurance
- Health insurance
- Risk insurance

### 2. Portal Operations

#### Personnel Access and Onboarding
Each personnel is assigned a unique MGM-Portal access code that enables them to create an account on the system.

The onboarding workflow works as follows:
1. A personnel receives an access code after successful shortlisting.
2. The admin verifies the personnel against their appointment letter and acceptance forms.
3. The admin completes onboarding using submitted documentation and personal details.
4. The system automatically generates the MGM-Portal access code and sends it to the personnel via email and SMS/WhatsApp.
5. The personnel uses the code to create a profile and access the portal.

#### Account Management
During account setup, the personnel selects an account type such as:
- Director
- Employee
- Contractor
- Partner

The admin then approves the application before full onboarding is completed and the personnel can proceed to use the platform.

## Key Features of the MGM-Portal

### Personnel Attendance Tracking
The system records:
- Clock-ins
- Clock-outs
- Attendance monitoring
- Working-time accountability

### Messaging and Collaboration
Personnel can communicate with each other within the portal through:
- Internal chats
- Messages
- Team discussions
- Virtual meetings or conference chats
- Media sharing such as photos, videos, PDFs, and URLs

### Personal Dashboard and Self-Service Access
Each personnel can view information related to their own account, including:
- Financial records and activities
- Salary and allowance payments
- Queries and responses
- Financial contribution management
- Insurance contribution management

### Profile Visibility and Privacy Controls
Personnel can view other personnel profiles as permitted by their privacy settings. The platform allows users to control what information is visible to others and restrict features such as:
- Chat
- Calls
- Contact visibility
- Other profile interactions

### Formal Communications and Requests
Personnel can submit notices and formal requests directly through the portal, including:
- Resignation notices
- Permission requests
- Memos
- Letters
- Attachments
- Other formal communications

These can be sent to the appropriate authority, such as:
- Head of Department
- Managing Director
- Chief Executive Officer
- Accountant
- Selected personnel or department contacts

## Intended Users

The system is designed for the following user groups:
- Directors
- Employees
- Contractors
- Partners
- Administrators
- Department heads
- Management staff

## System Objectives

The MGM-Portal aims to:
- Centralize personnel records
- Improve administrative efficiency
- Strengthen accountability and transparency
- Simplify onboarding and approvals
- Track attendance and work-related obligations
- Manage payroll, financial records, and contributions
- Support insurance and welfare administration
- Improve communication between personnel and management
- Reduce paperwork and manual reporting overhead

## Summary

Mobigate Group Management Portal is a digital workforce management and organizational administration platform that helps companies manage personnel, records, communication, attendance, contribution tracking, and formal administrative processes in a secure and structured environment. It is designed to support both staff self-service and management oversight, creating a more efficient and transparent company operations system.
